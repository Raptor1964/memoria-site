How security works

🔐 By default: maximum security

When you install Memoria, no personal data is collected and your master password is never stored anywhere. If you forget it, the vault is lost forever. It is your fortress, and you hold the only key.

📧 If you enable recovery (optional)

Only on your explicit request: you enter your email address and the app verifies it by sending you a one-time code. Once the code matches, the email is saved in the app, and your master password is kept encrypted on the device. If you ever request recovery, the app sends the password to that verified address. You can turn this feature on or off at any time.

Trade-off to be aware of: email is not a fully secure channel. Anyone who gains access to that mailbox could obtain your master password, so enable this option only if you accept that risk, and protect the mailbox with two-factor authentication.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Memoria really free?

Yes, completely. No subscription, no Pro version, zero ads.

Does my data end up on any server?

No. The database stays on your phone. The only external connections are the optional Google Drive backup, managed directly by you, and, only if you enable recovery, the verification code and recovery email.

What happens if I forget my password?

If you have not enabled email recovery, the vault is unrecoverable. That is the price of real security.

Yes, the app can be used for both personal and professional purposes.
