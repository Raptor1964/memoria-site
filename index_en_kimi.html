How security works

🔐 By default: maximum security

When you install Memoria, no personal data is collected and your master password is never stored anywhere. If you forget it, the database is lost forever. It's your castle, you hold the only key.

📧 If you enable recovery (optional)

Only at your request, your password is securely stored in your device's Keystore/SecureStorage to allow email recovery. You can enable or disable this feature at any time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Memoria really free?

Yes, completely. No subscriptions, no Pro version, no ads.

Does my data end up on any server?

No. The database stays on your phone. The only external connection is the optional backup to Google Drive, managed directly by you.

What happens if I forget my password?

If you haven't enabled email recovery, the database is unrecoverable. This is the price of true security: not even the developer can access it.

Can I use Memoria for business purposes?

Yes, the app can be used for both personal and business use.
