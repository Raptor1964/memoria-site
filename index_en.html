How Security Works

🔐 Default Mode: Maximum Security

When you install Memoria, no personal data is collected, and your master password is never stored anywhere outside your local vault. If lost, the database cannot be recovered. It is your personal fortress, and you hold the sole key.

📧 Assisted Recovery Mode (Optional)

Only upon your explicit request, the password is encrypted and saved within your device's secure system (Keystore/SecureStorage) to enable email recovery. You can turn this feature on or off at any time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Memoria truly free?

Yes, completely. No subscription fees, no premium tiers, and zero advertisements.

Are my passwords stored on remote servers?

No. Your database stays on your local device. The only external connection is the optional backup to your personal Google Drive account, managed directly by you.

What happens if I forget my master password?

If you haven't enabled optional email recovery, your database is unrecoverable. This ensures complete privacy: not even the developer has access to your master password or vault.

Yes, the application can be used for both personal and professional needs.
