Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how personal data is handled when you use the mobile application Memoria: Password Manager (the "App"), developed by Giancarlo De Rosa.

1. General Principles and Data Collection

The App is designed around data minimization and local security. By default, the App does not send your vault data or any personal data to any server.

  • Vault data (accounts and passwords): everything you enter (usernames, passwords, notes, cards, documents) is stored exclusively on your device. The Developer has no access to it.
  • Email address (optional): providing an email address is optional and off by default. It is requested only if you choose to enable the optional Assisted Recovery feature.
  • Google account (optional): required only if you choose to enable the personal backup to Google Drive.

2. Use of Google Drive (OAuth Scope)

For the optional backup feature, the App requests access to the Google Drive API with the restricted scope:
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

What this means: the App can access, create and manage only the files and folders it has created itself. It has no access to any other file or personal document in your Google Drive.

3. Optional Account Recovery

By default, if you lose your Master Password, the local database is unrecoverable. If you choose to enable the optional recovery feature:

  • You enter your email address and the App verifies it by sending you a one-time code. Once the code matches, the address is saved in the App, on your device.
  • Your Master Password is kept encrypted on your device.
  • If you request recovery, the App sends the password to the verified email address.
  • You can turn this feature off at any time.

Please note: email is not a fully secure channel. Anyone who gains access to that mailbox could obtain your Master Password. Enable this option only if you accept this risk, protect the mailbox with two-factor authentication, and change your Master Password immediately after recovering it.

4. Network Connections

Apart from the optional features described above (Google Drive backup and recovery emails), the App may connect to the internet to download service icons (logos or favicons) shown next to your entries. Like any web request, this may reveal to the server contacted your IP address and the web address of the service the icon belongs to. No passwords, usernames, notes or other vault contents are sent.

5. Storage, Security and Encryption

  • Encryption: vault data is protected with AES-256-GCM encryption. The encryption key is derived from your Master Password with the Argon2id algorithm.
  • No tracking: the App does not use analytics tools, does not show ads and does not track user behavior.
  • No sharing with third parties: no personal or technical data is sold, transferred or shared with third parties for commercial purposes.

6. Terms of Use

The App may be used for personal or professional purposes in accordance with this policy and with the terms of service of the distribution store: Google Play Terms of Service.
