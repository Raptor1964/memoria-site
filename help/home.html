🏠 Account List

On this screen, you can view and manage all your stored services and accounts:

📊 COUNTER: Located at the top left, it displays the total number of entries currently stored in your database.

📂 SERVICE LIST: The main body shows your accounts. Each item includes an icon (custom or default) and the name of the service.

  • Single tap: Tap on any service to open its details page, where you can view passwords, notes, and other information.

⚙️ SETTINGS (Impostazioni): Located in the bottom left corner of the navigation bar, this button opens the settings menu to customize the app, change themes, or manage your account.

🔍 SEARCH (Ricerca): Located in the bottom right corner of the navigation bar, this button allows you to quickly search through your accounts.

➕ ADD ENTRY: The floating button located on the bottom right (just above the navigation bar) is the quick shortcut to add a new service to your database.
