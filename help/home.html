🏠 Account List

This is the home screen of Vault, where you can view and manage all your stored services and accounts:

⚙️ SETTINGS: Located in the top right of the header (gear icon), it opens the settings menu to customize the app, change themes, or manage your account.

ℹ️ INFO: The "i" icon next to the gear, in the top right, opens the app information page.

🔍 SEARCH (Cerca qui...): The search bar is always visible at the top, just below the header. Start typing and the list is filtered instantly by service name.

🗂️ CATEGORY FILTERS: Below the search bar, a row of icons lets you filter your entries by category. Each icon shows the number of entries it contains:

  • Grid icon (Tutti): all entries. The selected category is highlighted with a blue border.
  • Globe: websites and online accounts.
  • Card: payment cards.
  • Bank: bank accounts.
  • ID card: identity documents.
  • Note: secure notes.

Tap an icon to show only that category; tap the grid icon to go back to the full list.

📊 COUNTER: The line under the filters (e.g. Tutti · 77) shows the name of the selected category and how many entries it contains.

📂 SERVICE LIST: The main body shows your accounts in alphabetical order. Each item includes an icon (custom or default) and the name of the service.

  • Single tap: Tap on any service to open its details page, where you can view passwords, notes, and other information.

➕ ADD ENTRY: The blue floating + button on the bottom right is the quick shortcut to add a new service to your database.
